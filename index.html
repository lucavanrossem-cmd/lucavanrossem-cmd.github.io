About

lucavanrøssem is a Ghent-based DJ and producer operating at the harder edge of electronic music. His sound bridges hard techno with hardcore and raw influences — built on precision, atmosphere and high-energy four-to-the-floor rhythms that hit hard but keep building, engineered to carry a dancefloor all night long. Explosive when it needs to be, progressive when it counts.

His tracks have drawn support from OGUZ, Restricted, Jazzy, Chaos Project, A.N.I, TITI and Karah, and over the past two years he's brought his sound to dancefloors across Belgium and internationally to Malta.

As co-founder of Sinners Culture and its label Sinners Records, he folds his own productions straight into his sets. Two and a half years into producing, his debut release “NEVER GAVE A FXCK” has passed half a million streams on Spotify.
